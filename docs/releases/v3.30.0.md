Release v3.30.0. The solar time-of-day bucket starts at a higher sun, 25 degrees instead of 15, so the morning ramp no longer shares a calibration with the middle of the day.

## Solar time-of-day bucket starts at 25 degrees of solar elevation (PR #268, #267)

Every interval is calibrated in a bucket for its horizon and time of day, and the time of day comes from the sun's elevation in the region. With the line at 15 degrees, spring moved the morning ramp into the solar bucket: in Brisbane, 06:30 crossed it on about 10 October and 07:00 a month earlier. Those intervals price differently from the middle of the day, and they blurred the solar calibration.

- **`solar`:** the sun more than 25 degrees above the horizon, outside the 16:00 to 21:00 peak.
- **`morning_ramp`:** 0 to 25 degrees.
- **`shoulder` and `peak`:** unchanged.
- **Buckets:** none added.

In Brisbane on 18 October:

| Time | Sun height | 3.29.0 | 3.30.0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 06:30 | 17 degrees | solar | morning ramp |
| 07:00 | 24 degrees | solar | morning ramp |
| 07:30 | 30 degrees | solar | solar |
| 15:30 | 32 degrees | solar | solar |

## Evidence

QLD1 stage 1 rebuilt from NEMWEB data (the 180 PD7DAY runs from 20 Aug to 18 Oct, and settled 5-minute prices), with an out-of-sample backtest over 6 rolling 5-day windows and every horizon (30,226 rows):

| Rule | All | 05:00 to 08:30 | 09:00 to 14:30 | 15:00 to 15:30 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 degrees (3.29.0) | 2.904c | 3.109c | 3.461c | 3.850c |
| 25 degrees (3.30.0) | 2.865c | 3.112c | 3.349c | 3.609c |

It is better in 5 of 6 windows; the sixth is level (+0.001c).

Ending solar by clock at 15:00 was tested on top of the higher threshold and was worse wherever 15:00 to 16:00 went (into peak, shoulder, morning ramp or a bucket of its own), so it is not included.

## Upgrade notes

- **Values:** calibrated values move from about 06:00 to 15:30 as the solar and morning-ramp buckets refit. The calibration refits on startup, so no action is needed.
- **Attributes:** nothing is added, removed or renamed. The bias chart's axis labels show the new threshold.
- **Requirements:** unchanged (`aemo-to-tariff` 0.7.28).

## Minor

A change to how intervals are bucketed for calibration, so a minor version. The suite goes from 1764 to 1768 tests.
