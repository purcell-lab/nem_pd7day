One PR since v3.8.0: #121, closing #120. Suite goes from 1016 to 1017 tests.

## One change, and it moves published values

v3.8.0 passed a raw forecast below the bucket's fitted domain straight through. Verified on the live install straight after that deploy, SA1 behaved as intended, but on QLD1 38 of 330 intervals became passthroughs: the 08:00 to 10:00 rows of every day in the run, with raw forecasts between -0.071 and +0.037 $/kWh, because the `h00_06__shoulder` bucket (domain floor 0.0398) and the `morning_ramp` buckets (floor 0.0) had never been trained on a forecast that low. A forecast a few tenths of a cent higher in the same bucket was calibrated. The domain floor is a sample minimum sitting at the dense edge of the data, so the step that #117 removed at -$100/MWh had moved to the floor, and the lower bound was clamped onto the raw point on 39 QLD1 rows.

Below the domain the published value is now AEMO's forecast shifted by the correction measured at the domain edge, `iso(x_min) - x_min`, exposed as `BucketModel.edge_offset`. It is continuous at the floor, keeps AEMO's slope below it so a deep negative stays deep, and uses the same first-step level that the isotonic clip already relies on, so it introduces no new estimate or constant. The band still comes from the stage 1 quantile lines. The `calibrated_source` label is `isotonic_below_domain` in place of `passthrough_below_domain`, since the value is no longer raw. The stage 2 gate and the stage 2 training exclusion are unchanged and stay keyed on the domain.

## What to expect on deploy

Intervals whose raw forecast is below their bucket's domain floor carry the edge correction instead of the raw value, so the published curve no longer steps at the floor. On the live install that is the QLD1 morning ramps and a handful of SA1 intervals. Automations keyed on `calibrated_source == "passthrough_below_domain"` must switch to `isotonic_below_domain`.

## Unchanged

The market price floor, the leverage screen, the stage 2 residual band construction, the sign-disagreement rule, and every point estimate at or above 0.02 $/kWh in the fixture golden table, which is byte-identical to the capture made before #85.

## Patch rather than minor

A correction to the behaviour v3.8.0 introduced. No sensors added or removed, no entity ids changed, no tariff definitions, no new attributes, no new tuning constants and no requirements changed.
