Release v3.37.0. City and zone price proxy sensors, with each place's loss factor from its own load connection points. Stage 3 of #285.

## Zone price proxies (PR #301)

- **Options:**
  - *City and zone price proxies*: the region's preset cities, plus Home, Home Assistant's own location.
  - *Zone radius*: default 100 km.
  - *Home load connection point*: a dropdown of the region's load points as AEMO names them.
- **Sensor:** `<City> Zone Price Proxy` shows ROP × zone MLF + the median local price adjustment of the region's catalogue nodes within the radius, in $/kWh, raw. The five-minute forecast and the pre-dispatch forecast, to the end of the next trading day, follow the same rule per interval.
- **Zone MLF:** the mean MLF of the place's own load connection points.
  - **Source:** AEMO's FY2026-27 MLF report, bundled as `load_points.json`. It covers 513 load points, and every MLF matches MMSDM.
  - **Values:** Brisbane 1.0120 from 15 points, Sydney 1.0035, Melbourne 1.0004, Townsville 0.9998.
  - **Fallback:** without a load point, the nearby generation's MLF stands in.
- **Adjustment:** city load has no local price adjustment of its own, so nearby generation and storage nodes supply it. The median keeps one constrained unit from moving the zone.
- **Labelling:** always marked a proxy (`proxy: true`), with the load points and member nodes as attributes. It is not a settlement or retail price.
