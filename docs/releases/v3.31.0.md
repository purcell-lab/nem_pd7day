Release v3.31.0. The day 2-7 sensors no longer double the price at the seam where they continue an Amber forecast in HAEO 0.5, and they say how each value applies between points.

## Day 2-7 seam and interpolation mode (PR #271, #270)

The day 2-7 sensors start where the Amber forecast ends (#235), so a sum of the two covers the whole week. HAEO sums its price inputs on their joined timestamps. HAEO 0.5 also holds Amber's last price up to and including its end, so the day 2-7 point at that same instant was added to it, and the interpolation spread the doubled price over the hour around the seam.

The three day 2-7 sensors (spot, tariff, export tariff) now:

- **First point:** when the series starts at Amber's end, its first point is published one second after that end, for example `20:30:01` for the interval starting 20:30. The series still starts at Amber's end, `forecast_start` keeps the interval's own start, and a series started by the clock rule is published as before.
- **`interpolation_mode: previous`:** each value is the price of the 30-minute interval starting at its `time`, held until the next point, as the scarcity sensor already publishes.

## Upgrade notes

- **Attributes:** `interpolation_mode` is added to the three day 2-7 sensors. The first entry of their `forecast` reads one second after Amber's end when the series starts there. Nothing is removed or renamed.
- **Values:** prices are unchanged.
- **HAEO:** no configuration change. HAEO 0.4 reads the series as before; HAEO 0.5 no longer doubles the seam.
- **Requirements:** unchanged (`aemo-to-tariff` 0.7.28).

## Minor

A new attribute and a published-time change on the day 2-7 sensors, so a minor version. The suite goes from 1768 to 1777 tests.
