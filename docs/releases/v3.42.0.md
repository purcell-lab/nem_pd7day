Release v3.42.0: pre-dispatch forecasts are recorded against TradingIS actuals, stage 3a of the forecast timeline plan (#333, #337). This only records: no published value changes.

## Pre-dispatch recorded (PR #352)

- **What is kept:** each new pre-dispatch run's regional price forecast goes into a per-region forecast history. Each settled half hour's TradingIS price is paired with every kept forecast of it, as PD7DAY's are.
- **Why:** stage 3b will fit a pre-dispatch calibration on these observations once there are at least 14 days of them.
- **Thinning:** pre-dispatch runs 48 times a day. Every run is kept within 6 hours of an interval, every 2nd run from 6 to 12 hours, every 4th from 12 to 24 hours and every 8th beyond. That is about 25 observations per interval, 1,200 a day.
- **Storage:** each region has its own keys, `nem_pd7day.<region>.pd_forecast_history` (3 days) and the `pd_` daily observation segments. The PD7DAY stores are untouched.
- **Cap:** 100,000 observations per region (`MAX_TOTAL_OBS_PD`), about 83 days. At the cap that is about 50 MB per region on disk, as PD7DAY's log.
- **Diagnostics:** `Predispatch Binding Constraints` shows `pd_observations` and `pd_history_days`.

## Upgrade notes
