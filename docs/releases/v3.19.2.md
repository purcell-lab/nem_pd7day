Release v3.19.2. Grid notices that AEMO has resolved or replaced no longer stay active.

## Resolved and updated notices (#216, PR #217)

On 30 September 2026 the SA1 grid notices sensor showed 4 active notices when AEMO had 2 standing. A suspect LOR3 for 1 October (145396) had been resolved by 145397, and an MSL1 for 5 October (145393) had been replaced by an update (145409), but both were still counted.

| AEMO wording | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| "The suspect LOR3 condition advised in AEMO Electricity Market Notice No. 145396 has been reviewed and is now resolved" | stored as a new, zero-length LOR3 notice; 145396 stayed active | cancels 145396 |
| "...Market Notice No. 145078 is cancelled" | cancelled by date match, which can reach notices it never named (#182) | cancels 145078 by ID |
| "Update to Market Notice 145393" | a second notice alongside the first | replaces 145393; the update stays active with its revised period |

"Refer to Market Notice N" cancellations work as before. An update only replaces the notice it names, and nothing if that notice was never stored.

Across the 34 LOR and MSL notices from 19 July to 30 September 2026, 10 parse differently and nothing else moves. Eight of the ten are SA1 MSL updates, so SA1 MSL counts were inflated whenever AEMO issued an update. The grid notices sensor, the grid stress binary sensor and the forecast chart's notice bands all read the same list, so all three change together.

## What to expect on deploy

- Notices already stored are not re-parsed; the store keeps parsed notices, not their text, and the fetch cursor is past them. A resolved or replaced notice stored before the upgrade stays until its period ends. New notices are handled correctly from the first poll.
- The `notices` attribute on `grid_notices`, and `active_notices_7d` on `grid_stress`, gain a `supersedes_notice_id` key on every notice: the ID of the notice an update replaced, otherwise null. No other attribute changes.
- The notice store gains a `superseded_by` field on replaced notices. Older stored files load unchanged; no migration is needed.

## Known and not fixed here

`forecast_mw` is null on every current MSL notice: AEMO now writes "Minimum demand is forecast to be -89 MW", which the parser does not read. Recorded on #216.

## Patch

Behaviour fix, no new entities or options.
