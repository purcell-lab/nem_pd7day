Release v3.29.0. One day's prices can no longer set the calibrated value at ordinary prices: a thinly supported calibration step serves the raw forecast, or leans on the well-supported steps around it.

## A thin isotonic step serves raw at ordinary prices, or leans on its neighbours (PR #265, #264)

On 18 Oct 2026 the QLD1 forecast for Tue 20 Oct 06:30 served 16.9c on a raw 10.5c. In the `h24_48__solar` step covering raw 10.2 to 11.7c, 18 of 32 rows came from 13 Oct, when PD7DAY forecast 10 to 12c and the price settled at 17 to 32c. On the seven other days in that range it settled at 5 to 10c. The step spanned 8 days, so the 3.26.0 support rule (#247) pulled it back by only 20 per cent.

A step whose rows span fewer than 10 distinct days is now handled like this:

- **Raw forecast 0 to 15c:** it serves the raw forecast. At these prices PD7DAY is about as accurate as the calibration.
- **Elsewhere:** it is pulled in proportion toward the fully supported steps around it:
  - **between two supported steps:** the line joining them;
  - **above the last supported step** (the top of the map, where spike forecasts sit): that step's level;
  - **below the first supported step, or no supported step at all:** the P50 line, as before. Pulling a thin bottom step up toward the step above would turn mild negative forecasts positive.
- **Stage 2:** it keeps reading the previous map. It is fitted and served on that map, so the two stay consistent.

## Evidence

QLD1 stage 1 rebuilt from NEMWEB data (the 180 PD7DAY runs from 20 Aug to 18 Oct, and settled 5-minute prices) and refitted, with an out-of-sample backtest over 6 rolling 5-day windows and every bucket and horizon (30,226 rows):

| | 3.28.0 | 3.29.0 |
|---|---|---|
| Tue 20 Oct 06:30 (raw 10.5c) | 17.3c | 10.5c |
| Mean absolute error, all | 2.935c | 2.904c |
| Without 13 Oct | 2.655c | 2.645c |
| 06:00 to 16:00 | 3.309c | 3.233c |

It is better in 3 of the 6 windows, level in 1, and slightly worse in 2 (by 0.005c and 0.019c). Serving raw on every thin step was much worse (4.258c): thin steps sit mostly at the ends of the map, where raw is far off.

## Upgrade notes

- **Values:** on the rebuilt data, about 6 per cent of forecasts with a raw value of 0 to 15c now serve raw. That ranges from 0.1 per cent in some 96+ hour buckets to about a third in the 0 to 6 hour shoulder bucket. Thin top steps at high raw prices calibrate lower than before. A new install with only a few days of history serves raw at ordinary prices until its steps have enough days.
- **Attributes:** nothing is added, removed or renamed.
- **Requirements:** unchanged (`aemo-to-tariff` 0.7.28).

## Minor

A change to how calibrated values are fitted, so a minor version. The suite goes from 1761 to 1764 tests.
