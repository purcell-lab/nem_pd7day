Release v3.27.0. Pre-dispatch now shows the constraint-automation constraints behind the false price-cap forecasts of 15 and 16 October, with a ratio that flags them. The constraint and pre-dispatch refreshes can no longer stall silently.

## Pre-dispatch: unassigned constraints and the RHS ratio to dispatch (PR #258, #255)

On 15 and 16 Oct 2026 pre-dispatch priced QLD1 at or near the $23,200/MWh cap hours ahead, while dispatch stayed near $60/MWh. The cause was a control-room constraint-automation constraint, `CA_BRIS_...`, applied at about half its dispatch RHS. The `Predispatch Binding Constraints` sensor showed none of it (`top: []`, `max_marginal_value_mwh: 0`): the ID names no region, and the sensor listed only constraints with a region.

- **Unassigned constraints are listed beside the region's own.** The dispatch sensor already does this. They are not given a guessed region: `CA_BRIS` most likely names the Brisbane control room, not QLD1. The new attributes are:
  - `unassigned_constraints`, for the current period;
  - `unassigned`, per period, up to five, largest first;
  - `max_unassigned_marginal_value_mwh`, per period.

  The region's count, `constraints`, `top` and `max_marginal_value_mwh` are unchanged.
- **`rhs_vs_dispatch`** on every listed constraint: its RHS over the RHS of the same constraint in the latest dispatch interval. It is set when the constraint binds there too and that interval is under 15 minutes old. It ran 0.38–0.49 through the 16 Oct false spike and returned to about 1.0 when it cleared.

## Constraint and pre-dispatch refreshes cannot stall silently (PR #257, #256)

On 15 Oct 2026 the shared constraint coordinator stopped refreshing. All five region constraint sensors froze, with nothing logged, until the config entries were reloaded.

Both coordinators refresh after each dispatch poll, and they drop a notification while a refresh is running. A fetch with no timeout could therefore hold the refresh, and a shared NEMWEB request slot, indefinitely.

- **Timeouts:** constraint and pre-dispatch fetches now time out after 30 s for the listing and 60 s for the file, as PD7DAY fetches do. A timeout is retried and logged.
- **Stuck refreshes:** a refresh still running after 10 minutes is cancelled, with a warning, and a new one starts.

## Upgrade notes

- **Attributes:** the `Predispatch Binding Constraints` sensor gains `unassigned_constraints`, `max_unassigned_marginal_value_mwh`, per-period `unassigned` and `max_unassigned_marginal_value_mwh`, and `rhs_vs_dispatch` on each listed constraint. `unassigned_constraints` is not recorded. Nothing is removed or renamed.
- **Values:** no existing value moves.
- **Requirements:** unchanged (`aemo-to-tariff` 0.7.28).

## Minor

New attributes, so a minor version. The suite goes from 1753 to 1758 tests.
