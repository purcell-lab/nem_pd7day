Release v3.39.0. Node and zone prices are now (ROP + adjustment) × MLF, and every price says which formula it uses.

## The node price formula (PR #322, fixes #312)

- **The convention:** NEMDE refers every offer to the regional reference node by dividing it by the unit's MLF. AEMO's local price adjustment is therefore on that basis, like the ROP, so the MLF scales both: node price = (ROP + adjustment) × MLF. Until v3.38.0 it was ROP × MLF + adjustment.
- **The evidence:** checked against the bids in force on 31 Oct 2026 (NEMWEB's next-day dispatch and offer archives).
  - Of 34 dispatched, locally constrained unit intervals, 17 priced to the cent at one of the unit's own offer bands with the new formula. None did with the old one.
  - Example: METZSF1 (MLF 0.8657) sat at its −114.72 band all morning, where the old formula gave −141.4.
- **What changes:**
  - **Covered:** node and zone states, and their P5MIN and pre-dispatch forecasts.
  - **Unconstrained intervals:** unchanged, since the adjustment is 0.
  - **Constrained intervals:** move by adjustment × (1 − MLF). GSTONE1 at 16:05 on 31 Oct: −967.10 $/MWh, where it read −1044.92.

## Local price and node price side by side (fixes #313)

- **DUID sensor:** keeps AEMO's `local_price` (ROP + adjustment, at the regional reference node) with `local_price_formula`. It adds `node_price` and `node_price_mwh`, the same × the DUID's MLF at its connection point, with `node_price_formula`.
- **Node sensor:** adds `formula` and `adjustment_at_node_mwh` (adjustment × MLF). `static_price_mwh` + `adjustment_at_node_mwh` = the state.
- **Zone sensor:** adds `formula`.
- **README:** a short table of both definitions.

## Upgrade notes

- **Values:** node and zone prices change on constrained intervals; history recorded before the upgrade used the old formula.
- **New attributes:** `formula`, `adjustment_at_node_mwh`, `local_price_formula`, `node_price`, `node_price_mwh`, `node_price_formula`. No entities are added or removed.
- **Requirements:** unchanged.

## Minor

A change to published values and new attributes, so a minor version. The suite goes from 1960 to 1961 tests.
