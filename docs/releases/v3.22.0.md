Release v3.22.0. The day 2-7 sensors now start exactly where the Amber forecast ends, found automatically, so a price series that sums Amber with day 2-7 has no gap.

## Day 2-7 joins the Amber forecast (PR #236, #235)

The day 2-7 spot and tariff sensors used to start by the clock: after tomorrow 03:30 between 03:30 and 12:30 NEM, otherwise after now + 24 h. Amber's forecast does not follow that clock, so a consumer that sums the two, such as HAEO, read a price of about 0 in between:

- 30 minutes on most refreshes. On 8 October at 14:18, Amber Electric and Amber Express both ended at 14:00 the next day, and day 2-7 began at 14:30.
- About 9 hours just after 12:30, when the clock moved to now + 24 h before Amber had extended past 04:00.

Now:

- **Amber is found automatically.** Every enabled sensor of the Amber Electric and Amber Express integrations is found in the entity registry; those carrying a forecast give the end of their last interval (`end_time`, or Amber Express's last start + 30 min). Live, 4 of 18 Amber sensors carry a forecast, and all four end together.
- **The series starts at that end**, with no gap and no overlap. Should the integrations ever disagree, the earlier end is used: an interval priced twice is a safer error than one priced at 0.
- **The sensors follow Amber.** They re-write when its end moves, about every 30 minutes and at the 12:30 extension, not on every 5-minute Amber update.
- **No Amber, no change.** With no Amber integration, or none with a current forecast, the clock rule applies as before.
- `next_value`, `cheapest_2h_window`, `min_24h_value` and `max_24h_value` follow the new start.

## New attributes

On both day 2-7 sensors:

- `forecast_start`: the first interval listed;
- `forecast_start_source`: `amber` or `clock`.

## Optional override

**Amber forecast override** in the integration's options (`amber_forecast_entity`) joins one chosen Amber sensor instead of searching, for example with two Amber sites installed. Leave it empty.

## Upgrade notes

- **No configuration.** With Amber Electric or Amber Express installed, the day 2-7 sensors join it on the first start after upgrading. An Amber integration added later is found at the next reload.
- **Values move** only on installs with Amber: the day 2-7 forecast starts at Amber's end instead of the clock cutoff, typically 30 minutes earlier, and up to about 9 hours earlier just after 12:30.
- **Attributes**: `forecast_start` and `forecast_start_source` are added to the day 2-7 sensors. No entity is added, removed or renamed.
- **Requirements**: unchanged (`aemo-to-tariff` 0.7.28).

## Minor

A new behaviour with new attributes, so a minor version. Suite goes from 1667 to 1695 tests.
