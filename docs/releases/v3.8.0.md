## One change, and it moves published values

The deep-negative passthrough boundary is no longer a constant. Since 2.0.8 every raw forecast at or below -0.10 $/kWh was sent straight through uncalibrated, with p10, p50 and p90 all equal to the raw value, while a forecast just above it was calibrated by the isotonic model. On the live install at v3.7.0 the SA1 `h24_48__solar` bucket had been fitted on forecasts down to -0.31 $/kWh from 814 pairs, yet Saturday's solar trough (raw down to -0.188 $/kWh, 12:30 to 14:00) was published raw with a zero-width band, and intervals either side of it were corrected by about 30 per cent. There is no basis in the data for a step at -$100/MWh.

The boundary is now the bucket's own fitted domain. A raw forecast below the smallest forecast the bucket was trained on is extrapolation, so it passes through as AEMO's value, floored only at the -1.0 $/kWh market floor, labelled `passthrough_below_domain` (replacing `passthrough_negative`). Inside the domain, however negative, the isotonic fit is served like any other value. The passthrough now carries a band from the stage 1 quantile lines, which are linear and do extrapolate, so `band_source` reads `stage1_quantile` there; `raw_passthrough` remains only when no line is fitted. `BucketModel.is_below_domain` is the single definition read by the serving path, the stage 2 gate and the stage 2 training filter.

The old constant protected the stage 2 regression from an isolated deep-negative training row by accident (#79). That protection is now explicit: `fit_ols_stage2` screens rows whose hat leverage exceeds `STAGE2_LEVERAGE_MULTIPLE` (3.0) times the mean, refits once, and falls back below `OLS_MIN_OBS` as for any thin bucket. It is two-sided and data-driven: a lone mis-joined row at either end is dropped, a cluster of genuine deep negatives is kept. Per-bucket counts are in the stage 2 summary log.

## What to expect on deploy

On regions whose solar-bucket domain reaches below -0.10 $/kWh, intervals that were published raw with p10 == p90 become calibrated with a proper band. On the live install that is SA1 in the solar trough; QLD1 had no forecasts below -0.10 in the current run and is unaffected. Automations keyed on `calibrated_source == "passthrough_negative"` must switch to `passthrough_below_domain`.

## Unchanged

The market price floor, the stage 2 residual band construction, the sign-disagreement rule, and every point estimate at or above 0.02 $/kWh in the fixture golden table, which is byte-identical to the capture made before #85.

## Minor rather than patch

Published values move and a `calibrated_source` label is renamed.

New tuning constant `STAGE2_LEVERAGE_MULTIPLE`. No sensors added or removed, no entity ids changed, no tariff definitions, no new attributes and no requirements changed.
