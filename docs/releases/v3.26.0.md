Release v3.26.0. One day's prices can no longer set the calibrated forecast: each isotonic step is levelled at its median, and a step resting on few days falls back toward the bucket's P50.

## Isotonic steps: weighted median, and support counted in days (PR #252, #247)

Stage 1 calibrates each bucket with an isotonic map, a staircase of levels. Each level was the decay-weighted mean of its training rows, so a step built from one day's rows served that day to every forecast in its range. On 13 Oct (AEST) QLD1 published:

- **44.2 c at 21:00 and 23:00 for a raw 26-31 c.** The `h06_12__shoulder` top step was five rows from one night, the 9 Oct pre-dawn spike (36 c at 04:00, 84.7 c at 05:00). Realised prices at 21:00-23:30 average about 9 c, with a p90 near 12 c.
- **18.2 c at 13:00 on 14 Oct for a raw 8.9 c.** One day, the 13 Oct midday at 28-45 c, lifted `h24_48__solar` at a raw 10 c from 7.7 c to 17.85 c in a single refit. The realised midday mean is 2-5 c.

The band hid both: p90 was raised to the point estimate.

Now:

- **Median steps.** Each step is levelled at the weighted median of its rows. A few spike rows set a level only if they carry half its weight. Equal neighbouring steps are one step.
- **Support counted in days.** A step whose rows span fewer than 10 distinct days is pulled toward the bucket's P50 line in proportion: a step on one day moves a tenth of the way from P50 to its level. The map is then made monotone again.
- **P50, not raw, is the fallback.** Far-ahead spike forecasts stay compressed: a raw $2.92 or $9.99 in a peak bucket is still served near 12 c.
- **Diagnostics.** `iso_top` and `iso_bottom` in the calibration sensor's `summary` now carry the step's median `level`, the value `served` at its top and its `days`.

## Upgrade notes

- **Values**: calibrated values move wherever a step had few days of support or was pulled by a few extreme rows. In the test scenarios the largest move is 2.7 c and most are under 1.5 c. On the live install the moves are larger where single-day events had set a step, which is the point of the change.
- **Bands**: the band still always contains the point estimate (#69, #114).
- **Attributes**: `iso_top`/`iso_bottom` gain `served` and `days`. Nothing is removed or renamed.
- **Requirements**: unchanged (`aemo-to-tariff` 0.7.28).

## Minor

A change to how calibrated values are fitted, so a minor version. Suite goes from 1749 to 1753 tests.
