Release v3.19.1. The calibration fit is restructured inside; no fitted value, published price, band or label changes.

## Fitters

Every calibration the integration serves is fitted by two methods on `CalibrationEngine`: stage 1 (the isotonic model, least-squares line and quantile lines per bucket) and stage 2 (the nine-feature STPASA model with its leverage screen, residual quantiles and feature ranges). They had grown into bodies of 157 and 224 lines. They now live in `fitting.py` as `Stage1Fitter` and `Stage2Fitter`, one small function per step (spec 006, PR #211):

| Step | Function | Issues |
|---|---|---|
| Rolling 90-day window | `window_observations` | #109 |
| Exclusions, time-of-day keys, cap, decay weights | `partition_stage1` | |
| Least-squares line and isotonic model | `fit_stage1_bucket` | #110 |
| Quantile lines, slopes sorted and clamped | `fit_quantile_lines` | #103 |
| Stage 2 rows, joins and the below-domain drop | `stage2_rows` | #68, #79, #85, #117 |
| Stage 2 fit, leverage screen, ranges | `fit_stage2_bucket` | #72, #79, #117, #147 |

The stage 2 fitter now takes its stage 1 result as an input, so the known issues in how a refit hands stage 1 to stage 2 (#210, #213) become a one-line change in their own release.

## How it was checked

Before any code moved, 13 observation sets (20,610 observations) were fitted by the previous code and every stored field, isotonic breakpoint and log line recorded exactly, reaching every branch of the fit that has a known input. The same test passes on this release, as does the golden master. A full refit takes the same time as before.

Three behaviours found while writing the specification are kept exactly as they are and filed for their own changes: #208 (stage 1 trains on solar time-of-day buckets, serving looks them up by clock hour), #209 (a full bucket keeps its oldest rows) and #210. Two speed-ups for the refit are filed as #212 and #213.

## What to expect on deploy

- Every calibrated price, band and source label is as before, and the calibration sensors' fit summaries are unchanged in form.
- The fit's log lines keep their text and their logger, `custom_components.nem_pd7day.calibration_engine`.
- No entity, attribute or stored file changes.
