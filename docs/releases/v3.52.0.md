Release v3.52.0: zone price proxies follow AEMO's IPRR congestion zones (#387, closes #317).

## Changed

**One zone price proxy per AEMO congestion zone.** AEMO's Integrating Price-Responsive Resources (IPRR) rule groups the NEM into 19 congestion zones, the areas AEMO manages transmission congestion by. Its draft *Congestion Zone Mapping Tool* (31 July 2026, published for industry feedback with the IPRR trial participation guidelines consultation) maps every TNI to one. Until v3.51 a zone was a preset city and every catalogue node within 100 km; that radius crossed electrical areas, and v3.40.0 left out by hand the plant three presets should not have counted (#317). Now:

- **Each region has a sensor per congestion zone:** QLD1 5, NSW1 5, VIC1 2, SA1 4, TAS1 3. Each is named for its largest preset city, by load points, and lists the others in `cities`:

| Region | Zone (sensor) | Cities |
|---|---|---|
| QLD1 | `Q_SOUTHEAST` (Brisbane) | Brisbane, Gold Coast, Sunshine Coast |
| QLD1 | `Q_SOUTHWEST` (Toowoomba), `Q_CENTRAL` (Rockhampton), `Q_FARNORTH` (Cairns) | one each |
| QLD1 | `Q_NTH` (Townsville) | Townsville, Mackay |
| NSW1 | `N_SYDNEY` (Sydney) | Sydney, Newcastle, Wollongong |
| NSW1 | `N_CANBERRA` (Canberra), `N_SWNSW` (Wagga Wagga), `N_NORTH` (Tamworth), `N_CENWEST` (Orange) | one each |
| VIC1 | `V_MELBOURNE` (Melbourne) | Melbourne, Latrobe Valley, Geelong |
| VIC1 | `V_STATEGRID` (Bendigo) | Bendigo, Ballarat |
| SA1 | `S_ADELAIDE` (Adelaide), `S_NORTH` (Port Augusta), `S_SOUTHEAST` (Mount Gambier), `S_RIVERLAND` (Renmark, new) | one each |
| TAS1 | `T_SOUTH` (Hobart), `T_NORTH` (Launceston), `T_WEST` (Burnie) | one each |

- **Members** are the region's catalogue nodes whose TNI the tool maps to the zone. 584 of the catalogue's 754 nodes map to a zone of their own region. A node the tool does not map (aggregations, non-standard IDs), or maps to another region's zone, is a member of none, and is listed in `unmapped_nodes` with `unmapped_node_count`. Murray (`NMUR`) is in VIC1 but in `S_ADELAIDE` in the tool, and Meadowbank (`TMB1`) in TAS1 but in `N_SYDNEY`: both look like errors in the draft, and are left out rather than guessed.
- **#317's exclusions go:** Eraring, Vales Point and Colongra are members of `N_SYDNEY`, Wivenhoe of `Q_SOUTHEAST`, as the tool places them. The tool splits the Snowy's Tumut hydro: Jounama in `N_SWNSW` with Wagga Wagga, Tumut 1 and 2 in `N_CANBERRA`, and Blowering and Tumut 3 (`NBLW`, `NLTS`) in `N_NORTH`, which may be a draft error too. `excluded_members` and `exclusion_reason` are removed.
- **Zone MLF** is the mean of every load connection point the tool maps to the zone, no longer only the named city's: Brisbane's `Q_SOUTHEAST` 31 points, 1.0051 (Brisbane's own 15: 1.0120).
- **Home** is in the zone of its chosen load connection point, through the tool, else of its nearest mapped node; its members are that zone's, nearest Home first.
- **The zone radius option is removed.** A stored value is ignored.
- **New attributes:** `congestion_zone`, `congestion_zone_source` (`AEMO IPRR Congestion Zone Mapping Tool, 2026-07-31 (draft)`), `cities`, `unmapped_node_count`, `unmapped_nodes` (not recorded). `radius_km` is removed.

The mapping is bundled as `congestion_zones.json`, built by `scripts/build_congestion_zones.py` from the workbook, with its date and draft status. Nothing is fetched at run time. When AEMO finalises the tool the file is rebuilt; the SJP1 postcode rows (149, South Australia) are kept for a future use, since a catalogue node's TNI is never SJP1.

## Migration

The entity IDs change from city to zone:
- **Each zone keeps the sensor, and the history, of the city it is named for:** `sensor.nem_pd7day_qld1_brisbane_zone_price_proxy` becomes `sensor.nem_pd7day_qld1_q_southeast_zone_price_proxy`. An entity ID you renamed is kept.
- **The other cities' sensors are removed** at setup, since they share a zone with it: Gold Coast, Sunshine Coast, Mackay, Newcastle, Wollongong, Latrobe Valley, Geelong and Ballarat. A repair notice per region names those removed. Update any dashboard or automation that used them to the zone's sensor.
- Home's sensor is unchanged.
