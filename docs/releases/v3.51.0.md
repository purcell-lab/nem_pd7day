Release v3.51.0: pre-dispatch is calibrated by its own fit, by horizon band and time of day (#337 stage 3b).

## New

**Pre-dispatch's own stage 1 calibration.** Stage 3a (v3.42.1) has recorded every kept pre-dispatch forecast against the TradingIS actual. Once a region's record reaches back 14 days, each PD7DAY refit is followed by a pre-dispatch fit on those observations, in the executor:
- **Buckets:** the 3a thinning edges `[0, 1, 3, 6, 12, 24, 40]` h by solar time of day: `p00_01`, `p01_03`, `p03_06`, `p06_12`, `p12_24`, `p24_40`. A horizon of 40 h or more is in `p24_40`.
- **Model:** PD7DAY's stage 1, by its own code: the rolling window, the spike and intervention exclusions, the decay weights, the `MAX_OBS` cap, the quantile band, and the isotonic map with its below-domain answer (#123) and top clamp. There is no stage 2 (#333 decision 1).
- **Thin buckets:** a bucket with fewer than 200 rows, or rows on fewer than 5 days, serves the next shorter band at the same time of day.
- **Validation, on every refit:** the fit is first made without the newest 7 days and scored on them by band. A band is admitted only if it beats raw pre-dispatch there, and at `p24_40` the interim too (PD7DAY's `h24_48`, v3.50.4). A band that is not admitted serves nothing, so its periods keep the fallback.

**Serving.** `predispatch_calibration.serve` takes, in order:
1. pre-dispatch's own fit where the period's band is admitted, labelled `calibrated_source: pd_isotonic`, with its band and `n_obs`;
2. else the interim from 24 h, `pd7day_iso_h24_48`;
3. else `passthrough`.

Everything that reads pre-dispatch changes together, as in v3.50.4: the timeline, spot forecast, day 2-7, tariff and chart entities, and the node, zone and constraints sensors' `calibrated_rrp`. Raw `rrp`, and the node and zone `value`, stay raw. The chart's pre-dispatch line reads `Pre-dispatch (calibrated)` when the fit serves it.

**Diagnostics.** The Calibration sensor gains `pd_calibration`, not recorded:
- `status`: `not_fitted` until 14 days of history, else `fitted`;
- `admitted_bands`, and the holdout `validation` per band (MAE and bias for raw, the fit and the interim, `admitted`, `reason`);
- `buckets`, in the shape of `summary.buckets`.

## Backtest

`scripts/pd_backtest.py` rebuilds what a live install records and serves from NEMWEB's own archives:
- the weekly PredispatchIS and TradingIS archives, 11 Oct to 7 Nov 2026, and PD7DAY's morning runs from 17 Sep;
- each run thinned as 3a thins it, and paired with the actual a live install records;
- PD7DAY's stage 1 and pre-dispatch's fit made at each midnight of 25 Oct to 7 Nov, on what was known then, as a daily refit makes them;
- that day's periods scored against TradingIS, spikes left out as the fits leave them out.

## Upgrade notes

Nothing to do.
- The fit is held in memory and refitted at startup, so pre-dispatch is served by the interim, or passthrough, until the startup refit has run.
- On an install whose 3a record is younger than 14 days, `pd_calibration` says `not_fitted` until it is old enough.
