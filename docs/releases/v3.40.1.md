Release v3.40.1: Home has one zone, zones and local price sensors do less work per update, and the tariff timer leak is fixed. Stage 0 of the forecast timeline plan (#333) starts here.

## Home zone in one region (PR #332)

- **Before:** every entry built a Home zone wherever Home Assistant had a location, so a five-region install had five Home Zone Price Proxy sensors. Four of them priced the home at another region's prices.
- **Now:** Home belongs to the region of its nearest catalogued network node, the grid region that serves it. A Sunshine Coast home is QLD1's, Canberra is NSW1's, Mildura is VIC1's.
- **On upgrade:** the other entries' Home zone sensors are removed from the entity registry at setup.

## Less work per update

- **Zones (PR #331):**
  - each zone works out its stations once;
  - its pre-dispatch forecast is kept until a new run arrives, a period ends, or the run goes stale.

  Adelaide, the largest zone with 46 members, went from 5.0 ms to 0.81 ms per update in a local measurement.
- **P5MIN and pre-dispatch (PR #342):**
  - these coordinators now notify their sensors only of a new run, not after every dispatch poll;
  - a DUID, node or zone sensor wrote up to three times per poll before; it now writes once;
  - the pre-dispatch sensors take their per-poll heartbeat from the binding constraints coordinator, so they still move to the next period on time.

  Values are unchanged.

## Fixes

- **Tariff timer leak (PR #341):** each tariff sensor registered every half-hour timer's removal callback, adding 48 a day per sensor and never releasing them while the entity lived. It now holds one pending timer.
- **Memberless zones (PR #343):** a zone with no member nodes read `conflicting_rows`; it now reads `unknown_node`. This only happens with an empty catalogue.

## Tests (PR #343)

The golden master gains a scenario with real DispatchIS, P5MIN and pre-dispatch runs and a pinned catalogue, so the DUID, node, zone and pre-dispatch sensors' values are now pinned ahead of the forecast hub refactor.

## Upgrade notes

- **Removed entities:** Home zone sensors in entries of regions other than Home's are removed.
- **Unchanged:** everything else.
- **Requirements:** unchanged.

## Patch

Fixes and performance only. The suite goes from 1973 to 1984 tests.
