Release v3.51.1: a horizon band the holdout did not admit no longer borrows a shorter band's map (#337 stage 3b).

## Fixed

**A band that lost on the holdout was served by the next shorter band.** In v3.51.0 the fallback chain walked from the period's band to shorter ones and served the first admitted, usable bucket it found. It did this whether the period's own band was thin or had failed validation. On the main install on 16 November, NSW1 and QLD1's `p24_40` lost to the interim on the holdout:
- NSW1: 3.29 c against the interim's 2.94 c;
- QLD1: 2.87 c against 2.70 c.

Their periods 24 to 40 h ahead were then served by `p12_24`'s map, which had never been scored at that horizon, instead of by the interim that had won. The day 2-7 start was among them.

Now:
- A band the holdout does not admit serves nothing. Its periods take the fallback: the interim from 24 h, else passthrough.
- A shorter admitted band still stands in for a thin bucket of an admitted band, as before.

- **At 24 h and beyond, the interim now serves where it won,** and the NSW1 and QLD1 errors fall.
- **Elsewhere a shorter band's map happened to beat raw** on the days the band's own fit was not admitted, but nothing had checked that. Every band still beats raw, and `p24_40` beats the interim, in every region.
- **A possible follow-up:** score the shorter band's stand-in on the holdout too, so it can be admitted on its own record.
