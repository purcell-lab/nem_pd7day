Release v3.45.0: the spot forecast on the price timeline, stage 5a of the forecast timeline plan (#333, #339). The spot forecast sensor's and the day 2-7 sensor's `forecast` is now the region's price timeline from the current interval: the dispatch price being paid, P5MIN's 5-minute prices for the next hour, pre-dispatch to the end of the next trading day, then calibrated PD7DAY.

## `forecast` from the timeline

- **From now:** `forecast` starts at the interval running now. Before, it started at the PD7DAY run's first interval, up to five hours back, intervals that had ended included.
- **Mixed resolution:**
  - 5-minute entries for the current dispatch interval and the P5MIN hour;
  - 30-minute entries after that;
  - shorter entries where a half hour is split at a source's end.

  `interval_minutes` stays `30`, the PD7DAY resolution. Each entry's own length is its new `resolution_minutes`.
- **The same keys:**
  - A `pd7day` entry is the calibrated entry as before; where its half hour was split, its `time` moves to the split.
  - A `dispatch`, `p5min` or `predispatch` entry carries every key too:
    - `raw_value`, `calibrated` and `value` are its price;
    - `calibrated_source` is `actual`, `raw` or `passthrough`;
    - the band and the calibration's diagnostics (`p10`, `p50`, `p90`, `ols_mae`, `band_source`, `n_obs`, `horizon_hours`, `spike_credible`) are `null`.
  - Each entry adds `source` and `resolution_minutes`.
- **By time:**
  - `min_24h_value` and `max_24h_value` cover the 24 hours from the first entry.
  - `cheapest_2h_window` is the 2 hours with the lowest minute-weighted average, and the 2 hours must be covered in full. Before, it was any 4 entries in a row, even with a gap between them.
  - `next_value` is the first entry's value.
- **Day 2-7:** its window starts after the cutoff, about a day out. That is PD7DAY, or the last hours of pre-dispatch where pre-dispatch reaches past the cutoff.
- **Writes:**
  - The spot forecast sensor now writes when a new P5MIN or pre-dispatch run lands, as well as on each dispatch poll and half hour.
  - The day 2-7 sensor also writes on a new pre-dispatch run.
- **Cost:** the entries, min/max and cheapest window are built once per timeline in the shared forecast hub. The spot sensor's attributes take 0.05 ms per write, against 0.06 ms in v3.44.0.

## Upgrade notes

- **Templates and automations** reading `forecast`:
  - The first entry is now the current interval, not the run's first.
  - Entries are 5 or 30 minutes long; read `resolution_minutes` or `time`/`nemtime` rather than assume 30.
  - `p10`/`p90` are `null` on the near-term entries.
- **`cheapest_2h_window`:** its keys are unchanged. Over P5MIN, `points` is more than 4.
- **Unchanged:**
  - the tariff sensors, which stay PD7DAY's until stage 5b;
  - the chart camera, until stage 5c;
  - the state of every sensor;
  - the `timeline` attribute and the Price Timeline sensor.
- **Golden snapshots:** regenerated. Only the spot forecast and day 2-7 sensors' `forecast`, `next_value`, min/max and cheapest window differ.
  - Some fixtures thin later intervals to 2-hourly, so their day 2-7 `cheapest_2h_window` is now `null`: no 2 contiguous hours.
- **Requirements:** unchanged.

## Minor

Published attributes change meaning. The suite goes from 2059 to 2067 tests.
