Release v3.25.1. A fix for 3.25.0: the calibration sensor's attributes are recorded again.

## Calibration summary kept out of the recorder (PR #250)

3.25.0 added each isotonic map's end steps (`iso_top`, `iso_bottom`) to the calibration sensor's per-bucket `summary` (#248). That added about 4 KB per region and took the sensor's attributes to about 20.5 KB, past the recorder's 16 KB limit. On restart Home Assistant logged, for every region:

> State attributes for sensor.nem_pd7day_qld1_calibration exceed maximum size of 16384 bytes. This can cause database performance issues; Attributes will not be stored

From then on none of the sensor's attributes went into history, not even status, fit time or observation counts.

The `summary` is now an unrecorded attribute, as the integration already does for its `forecast`, `slots`, `notices` and `intervals` payloads:

- every other attribute of the calibration sensor is recorded again;
- the `summary`, including `iso_top` and `iso_bottom`, stays on the live state and in the diagnostics download;
- history no longer keeps the per-bucket summary. Before 3.25.0 it was kept only because it sat just under the limit.

## Upgrade notes

- **Attributes**: unchanged on the live state. The recorder no longer stores `summary` for the calibration sensors.
- **Values**: no forecast, calibration or band value moves.
- **Requirements**: unchanged (`aemo-to-tariff` 0.7.28).
