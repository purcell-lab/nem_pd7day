Release v3.7.0. The usual two files, `manifest.json` and the `README.md` version history row.

One PR since v3.6.0: #115, closing #114. Suite goes from 1003 to 1013 tests.

## One change, and it moves published values

The calibrated price is no longer floored at zero. The isotonic value was published as `max(iso, 0.0)` on the claim that a calibrated price cannot be negative. In the NEM it can; mild negatives are the normal solar-trough state, and the model is fitted on negative actuals like any other. On the live install at v3.6.0 about one interval in nine was published as exactly 0.0 (QLD1 36 of 330, SA1 37 of 330), hiding the sign that a battery or EV schedule needs.

The isotonic value is now published as fitted. The only floor left is the market price floor, -1.0 $/kWh, applied to the point estimate and to the band's lower bound in place of 0.0, so p10 can sit below a negative point estimate instead of being clamped up onto it.

Stage 2's `prediction <= 0.0` fallback, which existed to stop a positive stage 1 value being flipped negative and as a side effect stopped stage 2 publishing any negative, is now a sign-disagreement rule plus a guard below the market floor. Where stage 1 and stage 2 agree on sign, including both negative, stage 2 is served.

## What to expect on deploy

Roughly one interval in nine moves from 0.0 to a small negative on the next state write. Tariff sensors fall by the same amount, since they add network charges to the spot. `min_24h_value` and `cheapest_2h_window` will report negatives. Automations that treated zero as the floor should be checked.

## Unchanged

The -0.10 $/kWh deep-negative passthrough, the stage 2 residual band construction, and every point estimate at or above 0.02 $/kWh in the fixture golden table, which is byte-identical to the capture made before #85.

## Minor rather than patch

Published values move.

No sensors added or removed, no entity ids changed, no tariff definitions, no new attributes and no requirements changed.
