Release v3.21.0. Each interval is now calibrated from the bucket it was trained in, by solar elevation, so published calibrated prices move between about 05:00 and 10:00 and around the edges of the solar day. The dispatch price no longer drops intervals, MSL notices carry their forecast demand again, diagnostics are complete, and refits are faster.

## Calibration buckets by solar elevation (PR #233, #208)

Stage 1 has trained each observation into a time-of-day bucket by the sun's elevation in its region since v2.2.0, but published prices and stage 2 looked the bucket up by clock hour. The `morning_ramp` buckets were fitted and never used, and in Queensland 07:00 to 10:00, with the sun well up, was served by the night-time `shoulder` model. Every path now uses the bucket the interval was trained in:

- `peak`: 16:00 to 21:00 NEM time;
- `solar`: the sun above 15 degrees;
- `morning_ramp`: 0 to 15 degrees, which covers early morning and a low winter sun before 16:00;
- `shoulder`: otherwise.

`calibrated`, `p10`, `p50` and `p90` change for intervals whose clock and solar labels differ, and stage 2 values change where its retrained models apply. In the golden scenarios, between 6 and 79 of 90 to 185 entries per fitted scenario moved. The tariff sensors follow, because they price from the same calibrated spot.

## Calibration fitting (PR #224, #226, #227)

- **A full bucket keeps its newest rows** (#226). A bucket at the 5,000-row cap used to keep its oldest rows and drop the newest, which the time decay weights most. Live, `h96plus__shoulder` was at the cap in all five regions, so day 4+ shoulder values shift.
- **Stage 1 is fitted once per refit** (#227). Stage 2 now trains against the stage 1 fit actually published, inside the same 90-day window. Stage 2 coefficients shift at the last published decimal place.
- **Faster refits.** The solar label is computed once per interval rather than per observation (#224), and the repeated stage 1 fit is gone (#227). Measured at about 1.15 s of a 2.35 s TAS1 refit.

## Dispatch price (PR #222)

The 5-minute poll now waits for the interval ending at the next boundary, and retries up to three times, 15 s apart, when AEMO publishes late. Before, a late publication was lost for good. The scarcity sensor's `signal_samples` averaged 4.61 of 6 and should now read 6.

## Grid notices (PR #223)

MSL notices read `forecast_mw` from AEMO's current wording, "Minimum demand is forecast to be -89 MW", including negative values. Notices stored before the upgrade are not re-read.

## Diagnostics (PR #221, #232)

- `calibration_summary`, `stpasa_run_datetime` and `pd7day_run_datetime` are filled again. They had been null since setup moved to `entry.runtime_data` (#221).
- `aemo_to_tariff_restart_needed` is new.

## Library version check (PR #232, #207)

If another integration imported `aemo_to_tariff` before this one, and Home Assistant then installed this integration's pinned version during the same start, the library in memory is the previous version. When that may have happened:
- one warning is logged, asking for one more restart;
- `library_version` on the tariff sensors reads unknown rather than a version that may not be running.

The install time is read from the dist-info `RECORD`, because uv keeps the wheel's own timestamps on the other files.

## Upgrade notes

- **Values move**: calibrated prices and tariffs between about 05:00 and 10:00 and at the edges of the solar day, as described above. No entity is added, removed or renamed, and no option changes.
- **Attributes**: diagnostics gain `aemo_to_tariff_restart_needed`; `library_version` can read unknown in the case above.
- **Requirements**: unchanged (`aemo-to-tariff` 0.7.28).

## Minor

Published values change by design, so a minor version. Suite goes from 1638 to 1667 tests.
