## One change, and it moves published values

Below a bucket's fitted domain the calibration no longer relies on the raw forecast at all. v3.8.1 published AEMO's value shifted by the correction at the domain edge, which removed the step #120 found but let AEMO set the depth where the calibration has no evidence. The 13:00 PD7DAY run of 2026-09-04 put -0.667 $/kWh on SA1 for Thursday 10 September 13:30 and 14:00 at a 144 h horizon. The `h96plus__solar` bucket is fitted down to -0.1001 $/kWh with an edge level of -0.0714, no settled actual in the 90 day window is anywhere near -0.667, and the last 20 days of actuals at 13:30 have a 10th percentile of -$12.5/MWh, yet v3.8.1 published -0.639 with a band to +0.011, a -$639/MWh chart trough six days out.

The rule is now: if there is no settled actual in the window near the raw forecast, do not rely on the raw forecast. Below the domain the bucket answers as if the forecast were at its floor. The point is `iso(x_min)`, exposed as `BucketModel.edge_value`, and the band is the quantile lines evaluated at `x_min`, clamped to contain it. That is the isotonic model's own clip applied to the band as well as the point, so the published triple below the floor is the published triple at the floor: continuous, monotone, no constant, and independent of how deep AEMO went. The raw value stays available as `raw_rrp` on every forecast entry and on the chart's raw line. The `isotonic_below_domain` label, the stage 2 gate and the stage 2 training exclusion are unchanged.

## What to expect on deploy

Intervals whose raw forecast is below their bucket's domain floor publish the floor's point and band instead of the raw value plus the edge correction. On the live SA1 install the Thursday trough goes from -0.639 to about -0.071 $/kWh. A genuine deep-negative event that AEMO forecasts beyond the bucket's evidence is published at the edge level until the interval moves inside horizons whose buckets have deeper evidence (`h24_48__solar` reaches -0.3128 on SA1) and stage 2, which sees STPASA demand, takes over.

## Unchanged

The market price floor, the leverage screen, the stage 2 residual band construction, the sign-disagreement rule, and every point estimate at or above 0.02 $/kWh in the fixture golden table, which is byte-identical to the capture made before #85.

## Patch rather than minor

A refinement of the below-domain rule introduced in v3.8.0. No sensors added or removed, no entity ids changed, no tariff definitions, no new attributes, no new tuning constants, no label changes and no requirements changed.
