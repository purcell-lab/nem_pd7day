Release v3.28.0. Stage 2, the STPASA correction, can no longer publish a calibrated price above the stage-1 P90.

## Stage 2 never publishes above the stage-1 P90 (PR #262, #261)

Stage 2 adjusts the stage-1 calibrated price for intervals 22–120 hours out. Nothing compared its prediction with the stage-1 band, so it could publish a price above the stage-1 P90: the 90th percentile of what settled at that forecast level in that bucket. The QLD1 stage-2 models have an R² of 0.19–0.59, which is too weak to support predicting a top-decile price as the most likely outcome. In the 16 Oct QLD1 comparison of raw and calibrated forecasts, stage 2 produced 98 of the 142 intervals that were published 3c or more above raw.

- **A seventh serving check, `stage1_band`.** It runs last and refuses stage 2 when the prediction is above the stage-1 P90.
- **Refused, not capped.** The interval is served its stage-1 value, as on every other refusal. A point estimate sitting on its own P90 is rarely right either.
- **Which P90.** The published stage-1 P90, which already contains the stage-1 value, so stage 2 can always match stage 1. With no fitted P90 line, stage 2 is served as before.
- **Unchanged:** downward corrections.

## Upgrade notes

- **Values:** some intervals 22–120 hours out that stage 2 raised above the stage-1 P90 now show the stage-1 value, with `calibrated_source` `isotonic` instead of `isotonic+stpasa`. Tariff sensors built on those intervals move with them. In the golden-master scenarios, three QLD1 intervals and one NSW1 interval move down by 1.4 to 2.0c.
- **Attributes:** nothing is added, removed or renamed.
- **Requirements:** unchanged (`aemo-to-tariff` 0.7.28).

## Minor

A change to which calibrated value is served, so a minor version. The suite goes from 1758 to 1761 tests.
