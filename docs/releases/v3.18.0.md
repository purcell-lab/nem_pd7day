Release v3.18.0. The experimental QLD1 scarcity premium uses a rolling 30-minute dispatch signal.

## Scarcity premium signal

The premium's signal was the mean of all 36 QLD1 dispatch prices from 07:00 to 10:00, and it was used only when every one had been observed live. A restart or a missed interval that morning therefore left the sensor unavailable for the whole 10:00 to 14:00 application window: on 28 September it was unavailable from 11:18, after a restart at 11:17.

The signal is now the mean of the QLD1 dispatch prices from ELEC_NEM_SUMMARY, which the integration already polls every five minutes, whose settlement ends fall in the last 30 minutes (PR #201). It is used while its newest price is at most 10 minutes old and recomputed on every dispatch update, so through the window the floor follows the last half hour of the market. One fresh price is enough to make the sensor available.

Unchanged: the $30/MWh trigger, the $65/MWh floor and premium caps, the 10:00 to 14:00 application window and the forecast series HAEO reads.

## What to expect on deploy

- During 10:00 to 14:00 the sensor shows a value whenever a dispatch price has arrived in the last 10 minutes, including straight after a restart.
- Attributes renamed: `morning_samples` is now `signal_samples`, `morning_mean_mwh` is now `signal_mean_mwh`. `required_samples` and `signal_window_nem` are gone; `signal_window_minutes`, `signal_freshness_minutes` and `signal_source` are new.
- Statuses renamed: `waiting_for_morning` is now `before_window`, `incomplete_morning` is now `no_recent_dispatch`.
- Samples already stored from a morning window age out on the first update.
- No other entity changes.

## Minor

A behaviour change to an experimental sensor, with renamed attributes.
