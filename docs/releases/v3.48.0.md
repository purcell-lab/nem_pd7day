Release v3.48.0: the scarcity premium and the local and constraint sensors on the price timeline, stage 5d of the forecast timeline plan (#333, #339). It is the last stage-5 consumer change.

## One served pre-dispatch price

- **One function:** `predispatch_calibration.calibrated_rrp_mwh(region, end, rrp)` serves the pre-dispatch price for a period.
  - It is passthrough today: the RRP itself, `calibrated_source: passthrough`.
  - Stage 3b (#337) will replace its body with the calibration fitted on the observations stage 3a records.
- **Everything reads it,** so 3b changes them all at once:
  - the price timeline's `predispatch` points, which the spot forecast, day 2-7, tariff sensors and the chart draw;
  - the node and zone sensors' `predispatch_forecast`, where each entry gains `calibrated_rrp_mwh`. Each entry's `value` stays on raw RRP, because the local adjustment comes from the same NEMDE run (#333 decision 3);
  - the pre-dispatch constraints sensor, which gains `calibrated_rrp` beside its `rrp` (raw, $/MWh), on the sensor and on each `forecast` row.
- **Values today:** every new field equals the raw RRP it sits beside. In the golden snapshots, all 693 `predispatch_forecast` entries are unchanged apart from the added key, and every constraints row has `calibrated_rrp == rrp`.

## Scarcity premium

- **New base:** the QLD1 scarcity premium now prices against the region's price timeline by half hour, from a new `half_hour_values` helper:
  - the dispatch price and P5MIN's 5-minute prices folded into their half hours by minutes;
  - pre-dispatch;
  - the calibrated PD7DAY run the base sensor warms.
- **What it replaces:** the PD7DAY run alone. Inside today's 10:00-14:00 window, the near-term half hours now use the freshest market view.

## Upgrade notes

- **New attributes:**
  - `calibrated_rrp_mwh` on each `predispatch_forecast` entry of the node and zone sensors;
  - `calibrated_rrp` on the pre-dispatch constraints sensor and its `forecast` rows.

  None are recorded beyond what each sensor already records.
- **Unchanged:** every existing value, state and entity, and the requirements.
- **Golden snapshots:** regenerated. Only `nsw_local_prices`, the scenario with pre-dispatch, changes, by the new keys.
