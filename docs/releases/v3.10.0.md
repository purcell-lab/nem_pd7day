Release v3.10.0. Stage 2 is served only inside the feature range it was fitted on, the two spot sensors' 24-hour min and max now cover 24 hours, and the grid notices sensor reports when NEMWEB was actually polled. Published values move; see the deploy section.

## Stage 2 extrapolated outside its training feature range

SA1, run 07:30 on 8 September, Saturday 12 September 12:30 (horizon 101 h, bucket `h96plus__solar`): STPASA had scheduled demand collapsing to 24 MW, so `log_demand` was 3.18 against a training range of about 5.7 to 7.3, and the stage-2 regression, evaluated two to four natural-log units outside the range it was fitted on, published -0.864 $/kWh for a raw -0.10 that stage 1 put at -0.012, with a band of -0.905 to -0.850. The sign and floor gates both passed; nothing asked whether the features lay within the model's experience. The neighbouring rows had demand50 of -13 to -27 MW, a degenerate transform, and were refused only because their predictions happened to land below the floor (#147, PR #151).

`OlsModel` now carries each feature's training minimum and maximum, from the rows that survived the leverage screen, persisted beside the coefficients as the residual quantiles are. At serve time `CalibrationResult.apply` returns the stage-1 result when any feature is outside its range: #123's principle applied to stage 2, below the evidence, publish the evidence. No constants. A store written before this field loads with empty ranges and serves as before until the next fit rewrites it.

The STPASA transform is one helper, `stpasa_feature_values`, read by both the serving path and the observation log so the two cannot drift, and it returns no features for a demand50 below 1 MW, where `log_demand` is 0 for every value and `poe_spread_n` divides by a clamped 1.

The calibration sensor's `summary` gains a `stage2` map: per fitted bucket, `n_train`, `r2`, `resid_q10/q50/q90` and `feature_min`/`feature_max` keyed by feature name. Before this, none of these were on any sensor and diagnosing the row above took inference from its neighbours.

The divergence backstop proposed on the issue is deliberately not included: it would also refuse legitimate spike compression, and the feature gate covers the failure that was observed. Left open on #147.

## min_24h_value and max_24h_value covered the whole window

Both spot price sensors published the minimum and maximum of their whole window under those names: on the day 2 to 7 sensor up to six days, so the SA1 Saturday row above set `min_24h_value` for the week. The README documents the attributes as the next 24 hours and the hot-water example is built on that reading. Both sensors now take the first 24 hours of their own window, sized in intervals the same way the raw attribute is: the first 24 hours of the run on the day 1 to 7 sensor, the first 24 hours after the Amber Express cutoff on the day 2 to 7 sensor. `cheapest_2h_window` still searches the whole window by design (#148, PR #150).

## Grid notices last_fetched reported the last notice, not the last poll

`last_fetched` was set only when a relevant LOR or MSL notice was stored, or derived from the newest stored notice on load, so on a quiet grid it sat days old across every scheduled refresh and three restarts, and a broken poll would have looked identical. It is now stamped at the end of every successful NEMWEB poll and is `null` until the first poll of the session, as the PD7DAY staleness attributes are. The old meaning is published as `last_notice_issued_at`, and the poll cursor as `last_seen_notice_id`, so a moving cursor proves the poll is working when nothing relevant is found (#139, PR #149).

## What to expect on deploy

- Stage-2 intervals whose features sit outside their bucket's training range fall back to the isotonic value at the first state write after the next refit (stored models carry no ranges until then). The SA1 Saturday 12:30 row goes from -0.864 to about -0.012. Stage-2 coverage falls by that share; baseline before deploy, run 07:30 on 8 September, `isotonic+stpasa` per 330 intervals: QLD1 155, NSW1 171, VIC1 168, SA1 138, TAS1 168.
- `min_24h_value` and `max_24h_value` on both spot sensors move to the first-24-hour values on the next state write.
- Intervals with STPASA demand50 below 1 MW carry no STPASA features and are served by stage 1.
- New attributes: `summary.stage2` on the calibration sensor; `last_notice_issued_at` and `last_seen_notice_id` on the grid notices sensor. `last_fetched` there is `null` until the first poll after restart.

No sensors added or removed, no entity ids changed, no tariff definitions and no requirements changed. Suite goes from 1049 to 1064 tests.
