## Stage-2 coefficients on the calibration sensor

`summary.stage2` gains `coef` per fitted bucket, intercept first then the feature order of `feature_min`. The v3.10.0 gate refuses stage 2 when a feature is outside its training range, and #153 found it refusing on features whose excursion is tiny (`poe_spread_n` a hundredth below a range 0.05 wide, `log_solar` a hair above its spring maximum) as well as on the extrapolation it was built for. Deciding whether an excursion matters needs the coefficient it multiplies, and until now that was on no sensor: the analysis could say which feature was outside its range but not what that cost the prediction (#153, PR #154).

## What to expect on deploy

Ten more floats per fitted bucket on the calibration sensor's `summary`. No sensors added or removed, no entity ids changed, no published values change, no tariff definitions and no requirements changed.
