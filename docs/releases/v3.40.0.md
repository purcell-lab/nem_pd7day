Release v3.40.0. Every preset city now has a price proxy sensor, enabled by default. Zones count one vote per station and leave out plant outside a city's electrical area. Non-physical units leave the pickers, and the binding constraints sensor says what it counts.

## Every city price proxy, enabled by default (PR #329)

- **New sensors:** every preset city of the entry's region gets a `<City> Zone Price Proxy` sensor, enabled by default. Home gets one too wherever Home Assistant has a location.
  - QLD1 has 8 cities, NSW1 7, VIC1 5, SA1 3 and TAS1 3.
- **Removing one:** disable a city you don't want from its entity page, and it stays disabled.
- **Options:** the zone choice is gone from the options; only the radius and Home's load point remain.
- **Data use:** the zone sensors read P5MIN, so it is now fetched every 5 minutes. That is one small file, shared by every entry.

## Zone aggregation (PR #326, fixes #314 and #315)

- **One vote per station:** a station with several connection points now takes one vote, its members' median. Wivenhoe's four TNIs used to hold 4 of the Sunshine Coast zone's 6 votes.
- **Below three stations:** a median of two is their mean, so the nearest station's vote now stands alone. `adjustment_aggregate` reads `nearest_station`, with an `aggregation_note`; three or more stations read `station_median`.
- **New attributes:** `member_stations`, and each member's `station`.

## Outside a city's electrical area (PR #328, refs #317)

- **The interim fix:** three presets leave out plant in reach that sits behind other constraints than the city's load, matched by TNI.
  - Sydney: the Central Coast and Lake Macquarie plant (Eraring, Vales Point, Colongra).
  - Wagga Wagga: Tumut hydro (Blowering, Jounama).
  - The Sunshine Coast: Wivenhoe. It is left with Valdora and Woolooga, so it takes Valdora's adjustment.
- **New attributes:** `excluded_members` and `exclusion_reason`.

## Home's load point (PR #327, refs #316)

- **Dropdown:** the load points of the preset city nearest Home lead the list. AEMO publishes no coordinates for load points, so it can't be sorted by distance.
- **New attributes on the Home zone sensor:** `nearest_city`, `nearest_city_load_points`, `nearest_city_mlf` and `zone_mlf_vs_nearest_city`. For example, Woolooga (0.978) sits against the Sunshine Coast preset's Palmwoods (1.0132), −3.47%.

## Non-physical units (PR #323, fixes #311)

- **Left out of the pickers:** dummy generators, reserve traders, and VPP, demand response and ancillary service aggregations, 95 in the catalogue.
- **Already chosen ones:** they keep their sensor, marked `non_physical: true`, and a notice is logged once at setup.

## Binding constraints (PR #325, fixes #318)

- **`filter`:** a new attribute stating what the state counts.
- **`binding_nem_network` and `binding_nem_network_ids`:** show binding constraints NEM-wide other than FCAS, whatever region their ID names. So `L_PEC_X_6C_6G_6H_XST` now shows when every region reads 0.

## Upgrade notes

- **New entities:** the cities you hadn't chosen, enabled.
- **Zone values:** they change where the station vote or the exclusions apply. The Sunshine Coast and Home take their nearest station, and Sydney and Wagga Wagga drop their out-of-area plant.
- **Removed option:** the zone choice is no longer read.
- **Requirements:** unchanged.

## Minor

New entities and attributes and changed zone values, so a minor version. The suite goes from 1961 to 1973 tests.
