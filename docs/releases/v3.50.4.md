Release v3.50.4: pre-dispatch from 24 h ahead is served through PD7DAY's `h24_48` stage 1 model (#337, interim until stage 3b).

## Fixed

**Raw pre-dispatch broke the Amber to day 2-7 seam.** Day 2-7 starts where Amber's forecast ends. Its first points come from pre-dispatch, about 24 to 31 h into the run, and were served raw. Raw pre-dispatch reads well above the actual price at that horizon. On a live install on 15 November (QLD1, run 21:01):
- the first day 2-7 point, 16 Nov 21:30, was a 30.2 c spot, where PD7DAY's own fit at that horizon serves about 13 c;
- through the Energex 96200 / 96200X tariffs the seam stepped from Amber's 23.5 c import and 12.7 c export to 43.7 c and 31.1 c;
- a downstream optimiser saw a 31 c export after the next evening peak and kept its storage for it.

Now each pre-dispatch period at `24 <= horizon < 48` h is served through the region's PD7DAY stage 1 isotonic model:
- the `h24_48` bucket for the period's solar time of day, keyed as PD7DAY serves (#208);
- stage 1 only: no STPASA stage 2, no run features;
- labelled `calibrated_source: pd7day_iso_h24_48`, never to be taken for a pre-dispatch fit, with the model's band, `band_source` and `n_obs`.

Below 24 h pre-dispatch stays `passthrough`. It sits under Amber for every consumer that cuts at Amber, and PD7DAY's shorter buckets were fitted to PD7DAY's error, at horizons where pre-dispatch is much better. Without a PD7DAY fit for the region, or for the bucket, it is `passthrough` as before.

The horizon is the period start minus the run time, as stage 3a records it and PD7DAY measures its own.

## What changes

Everything that reads pre-dispatch reads it from `predispatch_calibration`, so all of it changes together:
- the Price Timeline, spot forecast and day 2-7 `forecast` entries, the tariff sensors priced from them, and the forecast chart;
- the node and zone sensors' `predispatch_forecast[].calibrated_rrp_mwh`, and the pre-dispatch constraints sensor's `calibrated_rrp`. Their raw `rrp` and the node and zone `value` stay on raw RRP (#333 decision 3).

Also:
- Every pre-dispatch point now carries `horizon_hours` and `raw_value`.
- The chart's pre-dispatch line reads `Pre-dispatch (24 h+ calibrated)` once any of its points is served through the model, else `Pre-dispatch (raw)` as before.
- A new golden scenario, `qld_days27_pd_seam`, pins the seam over the real 13:02 pre-dispatch run of 31 Oct 2026: the 13:00 period, 23.96 h ahead, stays raw; from the next, 24.46 h ahead, it is served through `h24_48`.

## Upgrade notes

Nothing to do. Values change only where pre-dispatch is 24 h or more ahead: the start of day 2-7, and the far end of the spot forecast's pre-dispatch span.
