Release v3.47.0: the forecast chart on the price timeline, stage 5c of the forecast timeline plan (#333, #339). The 7-day forecast chart camera now draws the region's price timeline from the current interval: the P5MIN 5-minute hour and the pre-dispatch span as their own lines, then the calibrated PD7DAY forecast. It reads the forecast sensors' calibrated memo rather than calibrating every interval itself.

## The chart

- **From now:** the chart starts at the current interval. Before, it started at the PD7DAY run's first interval.
- **Distinct spans:**
  - P5MIN's 5-minute prices are an orange line, `P5MIN 5-min (raw)`.
  - Pre-dispatch is a purple line, `Pre-dispatch (raw)`. It is passthrough until stage 3b calibrates it.
  - Each line joins the point before it, so the forecast line does not break at a source boundary.
  - Their legend entries appear only when those spans are present.
  - PD7DAY keeps its confidence-tiered styling and band.
- **The same PD7DAY:**
  - The chart's PD7DAY entries come from the calibrated forecast memo the forecast sensors warm off the loop, so the chart draws what they publish.
  - In the regenerated golden snapshots, every PD7DAY interval the chart draws (435 across the 15 scenarios) matches the camera's own calibration in v3.46.0. That covers the calibrated value, band, raw value, source, horizon and spike gate.
- **Spike callouts are unchanged.**
  - The chart's callouts read the raw covariate gate, which the sensors do not publish at short lead.
  - The memo now keeps that raw gate privately, as `_spike_gate`. `forecast_entries` strips it, so no sensor publishes it.

## Cost

- **No calibration in the camera while a warm is coming.**
  - A render waits for the region's memo to be current, up to 60 seconds, as the tariff sensors do since v3.46.0.
  - It then builds its entries on the event loop, where the shared forecast hub lives; the executor only renders.
  - The camera calibrates for itself, in the executor as before, only when no warm publishes the memo within the wait.
- **Code moves:**
  - The memo wait constant moves to `calibrated_forecast.MEMO_WAIT_SECONDS`.
  - The chart's timeline data and render path move to `_TimelineChartMixin` in `camera.py`.
  - The span drawing moves to `_draw_timeline_spans` in `forecast_chart.py`.
  - `render_forecast_chart` stays at its size baseline.

## Upgrade notes

- **Golden snapshots:** regenerated. Only the forecast chart camera entities change, in their `forecast_data` and `png_sha256`.
  - `qld_empty_store` keeps its PNG: its chart window and values come out the same. Only the entries gain `source` and `resolution_minutes`.
  - The golden snapshot now records what the chart draws: the memo path when the memo is current, else the fallback.
- **Unchanged:** every sensor attribute and state, and the requirements. matplotlib stays optional; without it, the chart is a placeholder as before.

## Minor

A visible change to the chart. The suite goes from 2080 to 2085 tests.
