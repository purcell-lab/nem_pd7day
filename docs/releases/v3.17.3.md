Release v3.17.3. A market notice cancellation that names a notice cancels only that notice.

## Referenced cancellations cancelled the whole day

The grid notice store applies AEMO cancellations two ways: by the notice id a cancellation names, and, for AEMO's PDPASA cancellations that name no notice, by region, type, level and date. It ran the date match even after the id match. AEMO's referenced format also carries a date, the time the cancellation takes effect ("Cancellation - Forecast MSL1 - VIC Region at 1400 hrs 13/02/2025. Refer to Market Notice 124467"), so a referenced cancellation also cancelled every other notice of the same type and level whose period started that day. The golden master showed it: cancelling LOR1 notice 150200 also cancelled 150215, a different LOR1 on the same day (#182).

The two paths are now exclusive: a named id cancels that notice only, and the date match serves cancellations that name none (PR #189).

## What to expect on deploy

- When AEMO cancels one forecast LOR or MSL notice by reference, other notices of the same level on the same day stay on the grid notices sensor, the grid stress counts and the forecast chart.
- Notices the old logic already marked cancelled in the stored notice file stay cancelled until they age out.
- No entity is added, removed or renamed; no price moves.
